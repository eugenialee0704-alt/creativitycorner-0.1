Hong Kong Baptist University, Informed Consent Statement for parents/guardians

[Developing an Online Platform to Cultivate Creativity in Primary School Students]

Your child is invited to participate in a research study on creativity training activity in a course "ITS3009 Transdisciplinary Knowledge Application and Transfer II" offered by Hong Kong Baptist University. The purpose of this study is to explore whether the creativity training online platform can cultivate creativity in primary school students.

INFORMATION

Your child is invited to participate in five training activities on the online platform and participate in a creativity test before and after the training.

The interview will be conducted by a student at the Hong Kong Baptist University. The completed test result will be used for research purposes. Only the interviewer and the course instructor will have access to the test result. The test result will be destroyed at the end of the course (i.e. June 1, 2026).

It will take about 105 minutes to take part in the training activity and complete the creativity test.

The study will recruit twenty to thirty persons aged 8 to 13.

BENEFITS

The study will help to understand how to cultivate the creativity of children studying at aged 8 to 13 by using the online platform.

CONFIDENTIALITY

Number code will be used as the identifier. Responses will be treated as confidential and anonymous. The video recording will be destroyed at the end of the course (i.e. June 1, 2026). The soft copies of the data will be kept for up to four years (i.e. June 6, 2030) and will be destroyed afterwards. The data will be used for academic research and publication without the identification of an individual. The publications related to the study will be stored in the HKBU repository.

COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE

For participating in this study your child will not receive any compensation.

CONTACT

If you feel your child have not been treated according to the descriptions in this form, or your rights as a participant in research have been violated during the course of this project, you may contact the Research Ethics Committee by email at hkbu_rec@hkbu.edu.hk or by mail to Graduate School, Hong Kong Baptist University, Kowloon Tong, Hong Kong. If you have any questions about the study, please contact Lee Wing Sze, Eugenia at 61108514 or 23234121@life.hkbu.edu.hk.

PARTICIPATION

Your child's participation in this study is voluntary; you may decline to participate without penalty. If your child decides to participate, he or she may withdraw from the study at any time without penalty and without loss of benefits to which he or she otherwise entitled. If he or she withdraws from the study before data collection is completed his or her data will be destroyed.

CONSENT

I have read and understand the above information. I have received a copy of this form. I agree to participate in this study.

香港浸會大學 家長/監護人同意書

【發展一個培養小學生創造力的網上平台】

您的孩子獲邀參與一項關於創意訓練活動的研究，該活動於香港浸會大學課程「ITS3009 跨學科知識應用與轉移 II 」中進行。本研究旨在探討創意培訓活動網上平台是否有助於培養兒童的創造力。

資料背景：

您的子女獲邀參與網上平台上的五項培訓活動，並於培訓前後參加創意測試。

訪談將由香港浸會大學的學生進行。測試結果將用於研究用途。只有訪問者及課程導師可查閱測試結果。測試結果將於課程結束後（即2026年6月1日）銷毀。

參與培訓活動及完成創意測試大約需時105分鐘。

本研究將招募二十至三十名 8 至 13 歲學生參與。

研究效益：

本研究將有助於了解如何利用網上平台培養 8 至 13 歲學生的創造力。

私隱保障：

參加者的身份只會用問卷編號作為識別，所有資料將會被保密，而且無法識別個人身分。數據的硬拷貝（即錄影資料）將在課程結束時銷毀（即2026年6月1日）。數據的軟拷貝（即文字記錄）將保存四年（即至2030年6月6日），之後將被銷毀。數據將被用於學術研究，及在不公開任何個人身分的情況下發布或出版，與研究有關的出版物將在香港浸會大學檔案內儲存。

報酬：

完成問卷後，參加者將不會得到任何金錢作為報酬。

聯絡資料：

如你認為研究過程不符合以上的描述，或研究進行時有損你的子女的權益，請與香港浸會大學研究的道德規範及安全守則委員會聯絡（電郵hkbu_rec@hkbu.edu.hk），或電郵聯絡香港浸會大學研究院。如您對本研究有任何疑問，歡迎致電 61108514 或電郵至 23234121@life.hkbu.edu.hk 與李詠思聯絡。

參與條款：

你的子女的參與屬自願性質，你可拒絕參與是項研究，且不會受到任何懲罰。如你的子女已決定參與，亦可在研究中途終止參與，不會受到任何懲罰，亦不會損害你的子女的權利。在這情況下，數據資料將被註銷。

同意及簽名：

我已閱讀及明白本資料內容，並收到本文件，同意參加此項研究。
